# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Retab project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Accepting feedback gracefully and assuming good intent.
- Focusing on what is best for users and the project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and they may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for conduct they judge to be inappropriate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through a private message on GitHub to [@YOUR-USERNAME](https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will keep the identity of the reporter confidential where possible.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when deciding on consequences:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning with clarity about the violation and a request for corrected behavior.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and a period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from community spaces for sustained patterns of violation or severe conduct.
